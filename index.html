Robotics Systems

From sensor to motion, and the AI that drives it

A robot only works when every layer works together. These are the two loops I build in: the robot itself, and the AI that runs on it.

The robotics stack

How a robot turns the world into motion.

Sense

Get clean data from LiDAR, cameras, ADCs and IR sensors, from SPI links between FPGA boards to a lensless diffuser camera on a quadrotor.

Perceive

Turn signals into understanding: image reconstruction, obstacle detection, AprilTag localization, OpenCV tracking and SLAM.

Plan

Decide what happens next with ROS 2 navigation, A* search, finite state machines and trajectory generation.

Control

Inverse kinematics for a 5-DOF arm, 2 kHz real-time control loops on FPGA, and servo-actuated grippers.

Validate

Test stands, teleoperated test cycles and failure-mode isolation until behavior is verified on the real system.

Integrating AI into robots

How a learned model goes from data to dependable behavior on hardware.

Collect

Capture and audit training data. At Amazon Robotics I built a dashboard that tracks the quality and coverage of robot arm training data.

Train

Build the right model for the job in PyTorch and TensorFlow: U-Net reconstruction, CNN classifiers, LSTM gesture models, and DQN and Dyna-Q agents.

Simulate

Iterate safely in Gazebo, Unreal Engine and custom RL environments, with a sim-to-real workflow designed to carry policies onto robots.

Deploy

Run inference on the robot itself, like the lensless perception pipeline on an NVIDIA Jetson aboard a quadrotor: low power, low latency, no cloud.

Evaluate

Measure learned behavior on hardware: high-DOF manipulation under teleoperation, isolating failure modes, and keeping arm behavior deterministic.

Why test on real hardware

Logic that passes in simulation can still fail across a 10-meter cable or a live arm sequence. From FPGA timing at Brooks to manipulation at Amazon, I validate on physical systems so performance is proven, not assumed.

Why run AI at the edge

A quadrotor can't carry a big camera or wait on the cloud. Our lensless system swapped the lens for scotch tape and ran reconstruction and obstacle detection onboard, keeping the robot light, fast and able to navigate in the dark.
