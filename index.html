Robotics Systems

The whole loop, from sensor to motion

A robot only works when every layer works together. These are the layers I've built, and how I think about each one.

Sense

Get clean data from LiDAR, cameras, ADCs and IR sensors, from SPI links between FPGA boards to landmark extraction with MediaPipe.

Perceive

Turn signals into understanding: CNN classification, AprilTag localization, OpenCV tracking, and depth estimation with a custom U-Net.

Plan

Decide what happens next with A* search, finite state machines, line following and trajectory generation.

Control

Inverse kinematics for a 5-DOF arm, real-time PWM control loops on FPGA, and servo-actuated grippers.

Validate

Purpose-built test stands, simulation in Unreal Engine, and hardware debugging until the behavior is verified on the real system.

Why test on real hardware

Logic that passes in simulation can still fail across a 10-meter cable. At Brooks I validate FPGA timing and control behavior on physical test stands, so performance is proven rather than assumed.

Why simulation still matters

At AVMI I built LiDAR and SLAM simulation in Unreal Engine that replicates real-world perception scenarios, so autonomy software can be tested on cases that are slow, costly or unsafe to stage physically.
